Noon is for
signature cakes.

  1. Chocolate Mousse — a dark cocoa sponge goes down first.
  2. Pineapple — golden vanilla sponge, cream spread thick.
  3. Butterscotch — a toasty cocoa-caramel layer.
  4. Mango — a sunny sponge for the tropical one.
  5. Rasamalai — the final vanilla layer, saffron-scented.
