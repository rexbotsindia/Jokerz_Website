## Client-supplied cake renders (Chapter 03)

Supplied directly by the client (originals in `source-images/client/`). They were cut out from their white backgrounds by `homepage/scripts/process-cake-assets.mjs`, which uses a border flood-fill so the cream is preserved. The cocoa and golden layers are sponge-only recolours of the chocolate and vanilla renders. The toppings sheet was split into individual cherries, rosettes and chocolate pieces, and only clean pieces are used. Output: `public/assets/cake/` (`layer-chocolate/vanilla/cocoa/golden`, `topping-*`, `black-forest-layered`, `black-forest-frosted`; index in `cake-assets.json`). The frosted Black Forest render is the after-the-magic cake: a plain-topped version (`black-forest-frosted-plain`, toppings smoothed away by the script) is revealed by the cream wave, and the toppings then land and cross-fade into the original render.
