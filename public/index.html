One joyful house

Three counters, one sweet habit.

Come for a croissant, stay for a boba, leave with a cake box. Jokerz brings the bakery, the bubble-tea bar and the café under one bright roof.

01 · Bakery

Laminated, layered, lovingly over-buttered.

Croissants, cookies, cupcakes and cakes by the slice — out of the oven before the doors open.

02 · Boba bar

Pearls cooked fresh, teas shaken to order.

Milk teas, fruit teas and creamy blends — choose your sweetness, your ice and your toppings.

03 · Café

A sunny seat, a good coffee, no rush.

Pastel corners, natural light and the smell of fresh bakes — made for catch-ups and slow afternoons.

Our craft

Made by hand, every morning.

Behind the colour is real technique. Doughs rest overnight, buttercream is whipped in small batches and every rosette is piped by hand.

  • Real butter, real fruit

    No shortcuts on the ingredients that matter.

  • Small batches
